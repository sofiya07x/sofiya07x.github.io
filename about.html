OUR STORY

Created With Love for Cats

Sofiya's Cat House was established in 2026 by Sofiya with a simple goal: to help people discover beautiful cats and welcoming companions for their families.

We believe choosing a cat should be a thoughtful experience. Every family and every cat is different, so we encourage potential owners to learn about a cat before making a decision.

Our business is based in Madanganj, Kishangarh, Rajasthan.
